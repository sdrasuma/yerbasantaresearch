PUT THIS SITE ONLINE WITH GITHUB PAGES (free)

1. Create a free account at github.com.
2. Click "+" (top right) > New repository. Name it, e.g. yerba-santa. Choose Public. Click Create repository.
3. On the new repository page click "uploading an existing file". Drag in index.html and the photos folder. Click Commit changes.
4. Go to Settings > Pages. Under "Build and deployment", Source = Deploy from a branch, Branch = main, folder = / (root). Save.
5. Wait 1-2 minutes and refresh. Your site is live at https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/yerba-santa/

CUSTOM DOMAIN
6. Buy a domain from a registrar (e.g. Cloudflare, Namecheap, Porkbun).
7. In the registrar's DNS settings add:
   A     @    185.199.108.153
   A     @    185.199.109.153
   A     @    185.199.110.153
   A     @    185.199.111.153
   CNAME www  YOUR-USERNAME.github.io
8. In GitHub: Settings > Pages > Custom domain, type your domain, Save.
9. When the DNS check passes (minutes to 48 hours), tick "Enforce HTTPS".

EDITING LATER
- Log in to github.com, open index.html, click the pencil icon, edit, Commit changes. The site updates in about a minute.
- Add photos: open the photos folder > Add file > Upload files.
- Or press the "." key on the repository page to open a full editor in your browser.
